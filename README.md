# 📊 Enterprise Commercial & Logistics 360° BI Suite

An end-to-end business intelligence solution developed in **Microsoft Power BI**, designed to provide deep executive insights into commercial performance, customer segmentation, product profitability, branch execution, and target attainment.

---

## 🚀 Executive Summary & Project Scope
This professional 5-page reporting suite transitions raw operational data into actionable strategic intelligence. It covers:
1. **Executive Overview:** Net Sales ($32.85M), Gross Profit ($12.09M), and Target Attainment tracking.
2. **Sales Trends & Channels:** Temporal growth patterns (+23.75% YoY) and channel dynamics (Partner, Wholesale, Online, Retail).
3. **Customer Segmentation & Pareto:** 1,200 active buyers, 100% repeat rate, and geographic market concentration.
4. **Product Profitability & ABC:** Category margins (Automotive leading at 43.58%) and SKU classification.
5. **Branch & Employee Execution:** Profit contribution and target achievement evaluation across 13 branches.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Skills Demonstrated
* **Data Modeling & Cleaning:** Star Schema design, Power Query transformations, relationship management, and data quality audits.
* **DAX Calculations:** Advanced measures for time intelligence, dynamic segment shares, cumulative Pareto calculations, and conditional formatting logic.
* **UI/UX Design:** Midnight Navy theme (`#0F172A`), strict semantic color-coding (Green for success/on-time, Red for risk/delay, Blue for volume), and a standardized 1920x1080 canvas layout.
* **Executive Reporting:** Comprehensive `Executive_Business_Report.pdf` documenting root causes, data caveats, and actionable recommendations.
